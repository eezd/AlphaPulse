An upstream stream interruption with *no* text, thinking or tool output is temporary; once any output arrives, the same request is never automatically replayed. The exact-wait UUID control only wakes that request. Cancellation, ownership loss and non-temporary errors are not silently requeued.

Single-news analysis now parses a draft, removes blank/repeated aliases and merges only keywords with the same canonical, kind and literal quote. Every map result is limited to 4,096 UTF-8 bytes, and evidence quotes must match the exact frozen title, summary or body span; final citations must come from verified maps. All segments and a worst-case final payload must pass context preflight before the first model call. An unfit segment, too many segments, overlarge map/final or missing source evidence fails explicitly. There is no truncation or recursive compression. ## Behavior and boundaries

Each news ID receives one automatic analysis: canonical keywords, aliases and original quotes; summary and facts; regions/assets; impact, uncertainty and conditional scenarios; importance 0–100 with server-derived 1–5 stars. The full stored article and source revision are frozen before the request. Source revisions show a warning and require an explicit new analysis version. No event assignment or repeated full-event research runs in the new worker.

The `Asia/Shanghai` schedule uses left-inclusive, right-exclusive windows:

| Kind | Date D covers | First material check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `morning` | D 00:00–09:00 | D 09:05 |
| `evening` | D 09:00–18:00 | D 18:05 |
| `intraday` | D 18:00–D+1 00:00 | D+1 00:05 |
| `daily` | D 00:00–D+1 00:00 | D+1 08:05 |

These are start times, not guaranteed completion deadlines. Imprecise/unreliable publication dates use first-seen time and retain that label. Available analysis cards belong to the window of their pinned successful version's frozen effective time; later source-time corrections cannot move an old analysis to another window. News without a successful analysis still counts toward missing coverage using its current source-time basis. Version selection and window selection share one database snapshot. New York's regular 09:30 open is converted with daylight saving time; this does not certify a holiday trading session.

Period reports consume all available analysis cards and an exact-window market snapshot. They make one direct submission when possible, otherwise at most 16 persisted map submissions and one final submission. A daily report reads its three pinned period-report versions and at most three pinned highlights from each (nine distinct original snapshots maximum). It makes one direct submission, or at most nine persisted highlight reviews plus one final submission. Every logical submission has at most one existing structural repair; there is no recursive compaction. Empty windows make no model requests. Success with identical input/rules/model fingerprints can be reused across manual report versions; explicitly choosing regeneration recomputes successful steps.

A missing-news fraction of at most 5% is allowed and disclosed. Missing at least 20% **and** at least ten news items blocks the report. Smaller gaps are checked every 15 minutes for at most one hour. Historical first-analysis backlog waits separately before starting that clock. Missing daily dependencies never fall back to rereading the whole day's articles. A configuration with actual active requests all waiting to retry and no success for ten minutes is labeled a suspected upstream fault. A blocked report needs manual supplementation after recovery. Material waits do not occupy model slots.

Live reports, live news, historical news and historical reports share the existing 1–16 slot pool (default 3), in that priority order. The installed `alphapulse.briefings.worker` entry keeps the same advisory lock and systemd role. Restarted or revoked claims are interrupted and cannot publish late results. Maintenance pauses admission while allowing owned in-flight work to finish; user pause explicitly revokes current claims.

Only existing Binance `spot` and `um` instruments are priced. Returns require every aligned one-minute candle from the window's first open to its last close; gaps show insufficient data. Perpetual contracts are labeled separately. A daily return uses the full daily window and does not add the period returns. Stock prices require cited news evidence; this release adds no stock quote provider. High-star news notifications and price-threshold alerts remain future work.
